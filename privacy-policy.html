Salati - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Salati ("the App") is an Islamic application that provides prayer times, Adhan notifications, Quran content, morning and evening Azkar, and Duas.

Information We Collect

Salati may use your device's location information, including approximate and precise location, to determine your location and provide relevant prayer times. Location access is used for the app's prayer-time functionality.

Advertising

Salati uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information such as an advertising identifier and device-related information in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising practices.

Personal Accounts

Salati does not require users to create an account and does not provide account registration or login.

Other Information

Salati does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, videos, SMS messages, calendar data, financial information, health information, or web browsing history through the app.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as Google AdMob and Android platform services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the App. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Salati is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
